BOOK REVIEW

Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

J K Rowling

In the fourth Harry Potter book, Harry's connection to the dark wizard Lord Voldemort grows stronger as strange events signal Voldemort's return. At Hogwarts, the dangerous Triwizard Tournament begins, and although only students aged 17 and older are eligible, 14-year-old Harry is mysteriously chosen to compete. As he faces deadly challenges, Harry must also confront the rising threat of Voldemort, making this a major turning point in the series.
